# Climate Risk Housing Valuation

## Project Overview

This project investigates how climate-related risks influence housing prices across U.S. counties using machine learning and explainable AI techniques.

The study combines housing market data with climate risk indicators to explore whether flood and hurricane risks are reflected in property valuations and how these relationships change over time.

This work is being developed as part of my research in data science and climate-aware housing valuation.

---

## Research Questions

- Do climate risks affect housing prices?
- Are flood and hurricane risks priced differently by housing markets?
- How does the impact of climate risk change over time?
- Can machine learning models improve climate-aware housing valuation?
